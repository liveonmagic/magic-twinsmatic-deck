## No physical ATLAS release exists

His Discogs discography (artist `3303670`) is four entries and **does not
include ATLAS at all**:

- *Troubleseason* (2013, CDr mixtape, self-released)
- *Sounds From Last Night* (2014, CD, 3 versions) — the only confirmed physical product
- *Nowhere* (2015, 7×File AAC EP, label `tsc`) — digital
- Busy P, Myd, Twinsmatic — *Paris Worldwide* (2026, File MP3 single, Ed Banger Records / Because Music)

So ATLAS is a digital release. **Do not put an "ATLAS vinyl" or "ATLAS CD" in
the Shop frame** — those products do not exist and inventing them breaks
[[rule_never_fabricate]].
